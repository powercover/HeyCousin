# Hey Cousin!

A World of Warcraft Forever addon that finds the players who share your surname.

WoW Forever has no realms. Every character has a first name and a surname, so many players share
a surname, like a family. Hey Cousin! finds your relatives and makes the family visible: a crest
in chat and tooltips, a pop-up when a cousin is near, a family window with a guild family tree,
and an opt-in family map.

**In development.** WoW Forever opens on November 4, 2026.

## Commands

- `/heycousin` or `/cousin`: the commands
- `/heycousin status`: what the game tells the addon about you
- `/heycousin debug`: the errors the addon caught, in chat
