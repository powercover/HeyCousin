**WoW Forever gave everyone a surname. Find out who else has yours.**

Hey Cousin! is the family finder for World of Warcraft Forever. One click shows everyone online with your surname. Then talk to them, group up, recruit them to your guild, and see them on your map.

**At a glance:**

- **One-click family finder:** everyone online with your exact surname, with level, zone and guild
- **Family chat:** `/fam` talks to your cousins, in any guild
- **Group finder:** post "Deadmines, need a healer" and cousins can ask to join
- **Family map:** cousins who share show on the world map and minimap
- **Crests on nameplates:** spot a cousin in a crowd
- **Guild recruiting:** invite the cousins who have no guild yet
- **"A cousin is near!"** A pop-up when you meet, and one click to wave hello
- **Your own family crest,** three looks, 12 languages, and no other addons needed

It's useful from your first minute, even if nobody else in your family has it yet: the finder, pop-ups, crests, guild recruiting and your family's history all work alone.

## One click finds your family

![One click finds your family](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2156/174/found-png.png)

One click searches the game's `/who` for your surname and lists everyone online with **exactly** that surname, on your faction, with their level, zone and guild. Open a cousin's page to **whisper** them, **invite** them to your group, or **join their layer**.

Your family grows by itself too: the addon also spots cousins around you, in chat and in your guild, and remembers every time you met: where, when, what they said and their gear.

![A page for every cousin: Whisper, Invite and Join their layer](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2156/175/page-png.png)

Want to see another family? **Look up any surname** with `/cousin find Surname`.

## "A cousin is near!"

A pop-up with their face and a sound the first time you meet, or every time if you want. Click it to target them. Click **Hey cousin!** and you wave and say "Hey cousin Mira!" out loud, so everyone around sees it.

A cousin on the other side of the war is the family's **black sheep**, with a pop-up of their own.

## Spot a cousin in a crowd

Your family's crests on nameplates, and a red one on a black sheep's enemy nameplate, so you know who you're fighting. Drag and resize the crest in the settings: while that page is open, the real nameplates around you show it too, so you see exactly where it goes. Tried on EllesmereUI's nameplates, and made to work with any other, such as Plater, ElvUI or Kui.

## The family map

Cousins who share where they are show as crests on the world map and the minimap, with their names. Click one to open their page. It works beside map addons like Questie, TomTom and HandyNotes, and on **OpenMap**'s stitched map.

**Sharing is off until you turn it on.** Then your guild and anyone with your surname who runs Hey Cousin! can see where you are, on your faction only. Never in a fight or a dungeon. Turn it off, and your crest leaves their maps at once.

## Play together

When your cousins have the addon too, they're marked **(uses addon)**, you see their own crests, and you can:

- **Talk:** `/fam` reaches every cousin online who has it, on your faction, in any guild. Only people with your surname see it: like a guild chat for your family.
- **Find a group:** say what you're looking for on the **Groups** tab. Cousins click **Join**, you get one Invite button.
- **Hear the news:** a line in chat when a cousin comes online or reaches a round level.

## The family race and portrait

Who's the highest level in your family? **The family race** ranks you and your cousins with the addon, and tells you when one passes you.

**The family portrait** puts your house on one card: crest, motto, numbers and the cousins you meet most. Made for a screenshot to share with your family or Discord.

## Build your house

![Make your own crest](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2156/176/crests-png.png)

- **Your crest:** shape, pattern, colors, a symbol and a motto, or roll the dice. It shows at the start of cousins' chat lines, in tooltips and on the minimap.
- **Your other characters** show in your family, and a crown marks the eldest cousin we know, by a good guess.
- **Stats:** how many relatives you have, who you meet most, and the surnames you see most.

Great for roleplay families, too.

## For guilds: recruit and make it a family

![Your guild as a family tree](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2156/177/tree-png.png)

- **Recruit:** the cousins with no guild, each with an Invite button and a welcome you write.
- **The family tree:** your guild as a family, each rank a generation. A guild master can give each rank a family word and write a welcome line, and every member with the addon gets them.

## You stay in control

- **Your words are yours:** no chat line, whisper you can read, invite or "Hey cousin!" goes out without your click.
- **Quiet:** every alert has a switch, and one switch turns off all family messages. The game's ignore list counts too.
- **Open about what it shares:** your addon quietly tells cousins' addons your class, level and crest, and that you run it: nothing `/who` doesn't already show, but that. Where you are stays private until you turn sharing on.
- **Light:** windows are built only when you open them, and nothing runs in the background unless it has to.
- **Easy to start:** a two-step setup, or skip it: everything has a good default, and everything is in the settings.

## Commands

- `/cousin`: your family
- `/fam`: talk to your cousins
- `/cousin groups`: cousins looking for a group
- `/cousin find Surname`: who's online with any surname
- `/cousin recruit`: invite the cousins who have no guild
- `/cousin tree`: your guild as a family tree
- `/cousin race`: the family race
- `/cousin portrait`: your family portrait
- `/cousin settings`: every choice, by what it's for
- `/cousin setup`: your crest and the main choices, step by step
- `/cousin test` and `/cousin test map`: try the pop-up and the map crests

12 languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Ukrainian, Korean, and Chinese (simplified and traditional).

## Still young

The game is new, and so is this. Family chat and the map are tested between real players. The guild features, groups, and the minimap and nameplate crests have had the least testing in the live game. If something looks wrong, or you try it with your nameplate addon (open the settings' Nameplates page to see), please tell me in the comments or on [GitHub](https://github.com/powercover/HeyCousin/issues).
