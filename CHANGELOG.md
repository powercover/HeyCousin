# Hey Cousin! 0.1.0

- **Finds your cousins**: players with your surname around you, in chat and in your guild.
- **"A cousin is near!"**: a pop-up with their face and a sound. Click it to target them,
  right-click it for your family. Black sheep (cousins on the other side) get their own.
- **Your crest** in chat, in tooltips and on the minimap.
- **The family window**: your relatives, who is near you now, stats, and a card for each cousin
  with Whisper, Invite and Join their layer.
- **The setup**: make your crest and motto, pick your alerts and your look.
- **Two looks**: Pixel and Smooth. `/cousin theme` switches.
- **Test mode on Retail**, with a made-up family.
