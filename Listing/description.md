![Hey Cousin!](IMAGE_EMBLEM)

**WoW Forever gave everyone a surname. Find out who else has yours.**

Hey Cousin! is the surname finder and family finder for World of Warcraft Forever. One click finds every player online who shares your surname: your last name, or second name. Then meet your cousins, play together, see them on the map, and turn your guild into a family.

## Find your family

![Find your family](IMAGE_FIND)

- **One-click surname finder:** everyone online with your exact surname, with their level, zone and guild. Look up any other family name with `/cousin find Surname`.
- **It finds them by itself too:** around you, in chat, in your group and in your guild, and it remembers every meeting.
- **"A cousin is near!"** A pop-up when you meet, "Long time no see!" after two weeks apart, and one click to wave hello.
- **Family reunions:** a banner when cousins are in your group.

## Play together

![Play together](IMAGE_TOGETHER)

With cousins who have the addon too, in any guild:

- **Family chat** (`/fam`) and a **family group finder** (LFG).
- **Calls for help**, with an arrow to you.
- **Who has it?** Ask the family for an item. **Crafters:** who makes what. **Quest buddies:** cousins near your level, on the same quests.

## See your family everywhere

![See your family everywhere](IMAGE_EVERYWHERE)

Your cousins' crests on the **world map**, the **minimap** and **nameplates** (made to work with any nameplate addon), in chat and in tooltips. Sharing where you are stays off until you turn it on.

## Build your house

![Build your house](IMAGE_HOUSE)

- **Your own crest and motto**, and a crest that grows with **family achievements**.
- **The family portrait:** your house on one card, for a screenshot.
- **For guilds:** recruit the cousins who have no guild, and see your guild as a **family tree**.

## You stay in control

- **Nothing goes out without your click:** no chat line, whisper, invite or wave.
- **Every alert and everything shared has a switch.** Mute any cousin, and bad words from cousins are masked.
- **Backup:** your whole family as one line of text, safe from a deleted WTF folder.
- **Light** and needs no other addons. Three looks, and 12 languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Ukrainian, Korean and Chinese.

**Commands:** `/cousin` your family · `/fam` family chat · `/cousin find Surname` · `/cousin settings` · `/cousin backup`
