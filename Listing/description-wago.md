**WoW Forever gave everyone a surname. Find out who else has yours.**

World of Warcraft Forever has no realms: every character has a first name and a surname, and many players share one. Hey Cousin! finds the players who share yours, and turns them into a family you can actually play with.

## Find my family

![One click finds your family](https://cdn.wago.io/gallery/HO2UjIkBq20Z4TB2bGA95Z8NBsl0raaFjRWZiMqi.png)

One click searches the game's `/who` for your surname and lists everyone online with **exactly** that surname, with their level, zone and guild. From a cousin's page you can **whisper** them, **invite** them or **join their layer**.

![A page for every cousin: Whisper, Invite and Join their layer](https://cdn.wago.io/gallery/LzjDreoTZii1dGhgdgtKZbmq9k0HoJQG9wHSsna7.png)

The game's `/who` shows your own faction and lists 50 people at most. If so many names look like yours that the list fills up, the next clicks look through the levels, so a cousin isn't hidden behind them.

## The family map

Cousins who share where they are show as crests on the world map, with their names. Click one to open their page. It works beside addons that keep the game's map, like Questie and TomTom, and on **OpenMap**'s stitched map.

**Sharing is off until you turn it on.** When it's on, your guild and anyone with your surname who runs Hey Cousin! can see where you are, every few seconds while you move. Never in a fight or a dungeon. The other faction's addon never joins your family's channel. Turn it off, and your crest leaves their maps at once.

## Your family, together

Cousins who have the addon find each other through a hidden family channel. They're marked **(uses addon)**, their own crests show, and you can:

- **Talk:** `/fam` reaches every cousin online who has the addon, in any guild.
- **Find a group:** say what you're looking for on the **Groups** tab. Cousins see it and can click **Join**. That asks you, and the invite stays your click.
- **Hear the news:** a line in chat when a cousin comes online or reaches a round level.

Your addon tells them your class, level and crest, and that you're online: nothing `/who` doesn't show, but that you run the addon. Each of these can be turned off in the settings, or all of them with one switch. The game's ignore list counts here too.

## And for fun

- **"A cousin is near!"** A pop-up with their face and a sound the first time you meet, or every time if you want. Click it to target them. A cousin on the other side of the war is the family's **black sheep**, with a pop-up of their own.
- **Your crest.** Make your own: shape, pattern, colors, a symbol, or roll the dice. It shows at the start of a cousin's chat lines, in their tooltip and on the minimap.
- **The family window.** Everyone you met or found, who is near you or online now, and a page for each cousin: every time you met, where, when and how, what they said, their gear when you were close.
- **The family tree.** Your guild as a family, each rank a generation. A guild where most share one surname is a family by itself, and its guild master can give each rank a family word and write a welcome line.
- **Recruit.** The cousins with no guild, each with an Invite button and a welcome you write. A whisper is never sent by itself.
- **Your other characters** show in your family, and a crown marks the eldest cousin we know, by a good guess.
- **Look up a family.** Who's online with any surname, not only yours.
- **Stats.** How many relatives you have, who you meet most, and the surnames you see most.

## Three looks

Pixel, Forever (the classic game's parchment) and Smooth. Switch any time in the settings, or with `/cousin theme`.

![Pixel, Forever and Smooth](https://cdn.wago.io/gallery/1wiWqHacqtD5SbhnZ36LuSg5db75LpdidglQ1f4G.png)

## Commands

- `/cousin`: your family
- `/cousin tree`: your guild as a family tree
- `/cousin groups`: cousins looking for a group
- `/cousin recruit`: invite the cousins who have no guild
- `/cousin find Surname`: who's online with any surname
- `/fam`: talk to every cousin online who has the addon
- `/cousin settings`: every choice, by what it's for
- `/cousin setup`: your crest and the main choices, step by step
- `/cousin test`: a pop-up to try
- `/cousin test map`: cousins on your map for two minutes, to try

12 languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Ukrainian, Korean, and Chinese (simplified and traditional). No other addons needed.

## Still young

The game is new, and so is this. The guild features and everything that goes through the family channel (chat, groups, news, the map beyond a guild) have had the least testing in the live game. If something looks wrong, please say so in the comments or on [GitHub](https://github.com/powercover/HeyCousin/issues).

The [source code](https://github.com/powercover/HeyCousin) is there too, so you can read exactly what the addon sends. All rights reserved.
